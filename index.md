The 13th edition of doctoral school on solid earth sciences will be held in Les Houches Physic School, in the French Alps, from October 12th to October 23th 2026.

This doctoral school trains PhD and Master students by covering most aspects of Solid Earth sciences, including seismology, mineralogy, geodynamics, geophysics and geochemistry. A field excursion is  proposed to link theoretical aspects to observations. This doctoral training welcomes students trained in geology, physics, chemistry, mathematics or computer science who are interested in the deep interior of the Earth (mantle and core).

Registration deadline September 10th 2026.
